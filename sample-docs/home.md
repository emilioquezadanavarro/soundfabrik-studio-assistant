Welcome to Master Sound Berlin, a fictional recording studio in the heart of the capital

Master Sound Berlin is a sample studio used to demo this project. Our two rooms span
roughly 300 square meters in a converted warehouse in Neukölln. Designed for
musicians, producers, and audio enthusiasts alike, Master Sound Berlin offers a relaxed
environment for tracking, mixing, and production.

With modern equipment and a small team of engineers, we offer recording, editing,
mixing, and mastering services for solo artists, bands, choirs, and ensembles.

Studio A

Recording, Rehearsals, Ensemble Sessions

The centerpiece of Studio A is a 70m² live room with a Yamaha C7 grand piano,
one isolation booth, and a control room built around a Solid State Logic Origin
console.

Studio B

Songwriting, Mixing, Immersive Audio

Studio B is a plug-and-play production and mixing room with a 5.1.4 immersive
monitoring setup, designed for composing, mixing, and Atmos work.
