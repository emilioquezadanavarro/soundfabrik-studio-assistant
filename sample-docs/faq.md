# Frequently Asked Questions

## Which room should I choose?

Studio A is our live room, built for full-band tracking, ensembles, and
rehearsals. Studio B is a plug-and-play production and mixing room, better
suited for songwriting, mixing, and immersive work. If you're not sure, tell us
what you're working on and we'll point you to the right room.

## Can I record a full band in Studio B?

Not really — Studio B is a control and listening room without its own live
room. Use Studio A for full-band tracking. Studio B can connect to the
Studio A isolation booth for overdubs or a single instrument.

## Can I bring my own gear or DAW?

Yes. Studio B is built around a plug-and-play workflow, and you're welcome
to bring your own laptop, instruments, or a preferred drum kit to either room.

## Do I get an engineer, or can I work on my own?

Both. You can book one of our engineers, or rent Studio B and work
independently. Let us know which you'd prefer when you enquire.

## How much does it cost?

Rates are quoted per project depending on the room, duration, and whether you
want an engineer. Share your project details along with your name and an email
or phone number and we'll send a quote.

## How do I book a session?

Send us your project details and preferred dates, plus your name and an email
or phone number. Lena Aumann, our studio manager, handles scheduling and will
follow up directly.

## Do you offer mixing or mastering only, without recording?

Yes — bring finished recordings for mixing and mastering, or book Studio B
to mix it yourself.

## Do you do immersive audio?

Yes, Studio B has a 5.1.4 immersive setup and we offer mixing in immersive
formats on request.

## Can I use the studio for rehearsals?

Yes, Studio A's live room is available as a rehearsal space for touring
bands. Contact us for details.
