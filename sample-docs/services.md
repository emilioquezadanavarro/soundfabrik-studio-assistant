# Services

# 1. Recording

With our two rooms and a wide selection of mics and instruments, we can tailor
our setup to most recording needs, from a solo singer-songwriter to a small
ensemble.

# 2. Immersive audio

Studio South's 5.1.4 setup gives your mix an extra dimension. We offer mixing
in immersive formats on request.

# 3. Mixing

Leave the mixing to one of our engineers, or rent Studio South to mix your own
production. Excellent acoustics and natural light make it a comfortable place
to work.

# 4. Rehearsals

Preparing for a tour? Studio North's live room is available as a rehearsal
space — just contact us for details.

# 5. Video & photo

The studio is occasionally used as a filming location for music videos and
photo shoots. Bring your own crew or let us film your session.

# 6. Events

With a screen, projector, and PA system, our live room combined with the
lounge is a good fit for small concerts, workshops, and listening sessions.
